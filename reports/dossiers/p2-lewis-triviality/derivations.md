## Conventions

- **Calculus:** natural deduction, Gentzen's NK. **Object logic:** `classical-hol` (problem card),
  read as typed higher-order logic. No step below needs a classical rule (RAA, excluded middle,
  double negation elimination). The only negation step is ¬E followed by ⊥E (ex falso, in R2's
  ∨E), and NJ admits both, so every derivation also holds intuitionistically.
- **Rules:** `hyp` (assumption); `∧I`, `∧E`; `→I` (discharging the cited `hyp` lines), `→E`;
  `∀I` (eigenvariable fresh: none of the variables generalized occurs free in an open hypothesis),
  `∀E`; `∨E` (major premise, then each case's hypothesis and conclusion); `¬E`, `⊥E`; `=E`
  (Leibniz substitution of the cited equations into the first cited line); `=trans` (transitivity
  and symmetry of `=`); `β` (β-conversion); `def N` (δ-unfolding or folding of the Lean definition
  `MathAgents.P2LewisTriviality.N`, a definitional equality).
- **Lemmas:** the Mathlib names below are all in `reports/kernel/name-index.txt` (pinned toolchain
  leanprover/lean4:v4.34.1). The `inf_*`, `bot_inf_eq` and `ne_of_gt` declarations are generated by
  `@[to_dual]`, and `left_eq_add` by `@[to_additive]`. The Aux lemmas are this dossier's blueprint
  lemmas L1–L8, in the namespace `MathAgents.P2LewisTriviality.Aux`, and are not yet declared.
- **Deviation from strategy.md:** strategy.md and its review cite `Set.mem_setOf_eq`. Under the
  pinned Mathlib that is a deprecated alias (since 2026-07-09) of `Set.mem_ofPred_eq`, and the name
  index lists only `Set.mem_ofPred_eq`, so the derivations cite `Set.mem_ofPred_eq`.
- **Notation:** `P x` is `P x : ℝ`. In L6 and R3, `P_B` abbreviates `fun X => condProb P X B`, and
  `F` in L8 and R3:all abbreviates `{P : α → ℝ | IsFinAddProb P}`. `Add P` abbreviates the
  additivity field `∀ a b, Disjoint a b → P (a ⊔ b) = P a + P b`.
