1. Scope. goose stays out of brief.json frozen_surfaces and out of write_policy_frameworks (agentteams refuses the
   switch on Goose: GOOSE_SWITCH_REFUSED). AGENTS.md, which Goose co-owns, is rendered with it.
2. Goose UNENFORCED, with the D34 widening accepted. Goose renders off the switch; read-only and command-running Goose
   recipes carry `developer` (Goose's only file tool, which also writes files and runs commands). The bespoke pipeline
   recipes stay grant-scoped by mathagents' scope-goose. The stage-agent audit reports the count as accepted risk
   (D34) and must report no other violation.
3. Not accepted. A recipe on a read-only or command-running agent gaining a grant beyond `developer` and `summon`;
   `analyze` other than disabled (`available_tools: [__none__]`); an `agentteams_readfs` server other than
   `python3 -I -S scripts/goose-readfs-mcp.py` matching agentteams' READFS_SHA256; a recipe whose stdio server file is
   missing; an orchestrator recipe whose sub_recipes name an agent absent from .github/agents.
3a. Goose sandbox control-plane gap, accepted knowingly. Off the switch, agentteams' Goose seatbelt profile
   (.goose/sandbox.sb) loses the write-denies on .agentteams, .agentteams-status and .goose/recipes, so a Goose session
   with a `developer` shell could edit the Codex role gate or its own recipes. Accepted until agentteams protects the
   shared control plane in the Goose profile whenever any surface is orchestrator-only. Detection only, at the next
   render (refresh step 2 checks the runner and readfs servers against agentteams' pinned sha256; the render check
   re-renders the role gate and diffs it).
4. Review. By 2027-02-09 the operator re-freezes Goose, moves it under the switch once agentteams enforces it there, or
   records a new signed decision. There is no silent renewal. The registry entry expires then, and an expired active
   entry makes agentteams refuse every new relaxing activation until it is retired.
5. Toolchain. Effective only with agentteams v1.1.0 or later and researchteam v1.1.0 or later, release tags pinned in
   toolchain.lock.
6. Use. Renders go through scripts/refresh_agent_surfaces.sh only. researchteam's unfreeze check does not consume this
   row; it stays in force until item 4's date, a HALT, or a superseding row.
